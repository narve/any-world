# M4 — Polish & modes: progress note

Date: 2026-10-24. Status: **exit check met.** `npm run build:static` produces a browser-only build that plays the recorded worlds with no key and no network, and both deployment modes are covered by e2e (`e2e/modes.spec.ts`). New in M4: generative audio with an optional SoundCloud panel; a phone and tablet layout with touch input; a full Norwegian bokmål UI with two translated demo worlds; and an app shell with provider status, world files, an offline demo generator and a README. Typecheck, vitest, validate-worlds, the sim smoke, both builds and Playwright (desktop, phone and tablet, plus the Norwegian playthrough) are green.

## What was built

| Area | Where | Notes |

## Known issues (to M5)

- **Real devices:** touch feel, frame rate, safe areas on notched phones, iOS Safari gesture events and audio unlock are only emulated in Chromium. They need a check on a real phone.
- **Phone portrait leaves an empty band** between the goal line and the map on tall screens (see `mobile-iphone13-portrait-2-play-1.png`). The camera insets keep the player clear of panels but do not pull the map up.
- **Main chunk is 826 kB (268 kB gzip).** It still holds the LLM client, zod schemas, both catalogs and the prompt library. Splitting the LLM client and the nb catalog out is the next step if first load matters.
- **Two mute controls:** the music panel's Mute and volume act on the live audio engine, while the settings checkbox is the saved preference. They are not synced in both directions.
- **The SoundCloud player can only embed a direct track or playlist link.** AI suggestions are usually plain text and appear as search links.
- **Landscape context column** shows about 3–4 buttons; any more scroll.
- **No e2e covers `npm start`** (production server serving `dist/client`). Static serving is covered through `serve-static`.
- **README links `RETROSPECTIVE.md`**, which is written in M5.
- **Norwegian grammar** depends on worlds writing verbs in the present tense (the engine passes world words into catalog templates without inflecting them).
