# M2 — Systems, non-realistic worlds, GM rules, balance: progress note

Date: 2026-10-24. Status: **exit check met.** All 4 fixture worlds play end-to-end in the browser. Every bot (explorer, cautious, talker, random) runs on every fixture with 0 crashes and 0 soft-locks. Explorer win rates on `normal` are inside the 25–60% band for all four worlds. Typecheck, unit, e2e and world validation are green.

## What was built

| Area | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Non-realistic mapgen | `src/engine/mapgen/generators/{islands,organic,geometric}.ts` | `islands` gives floating platforms over a void, `organic` gives membranes and blobs, and `geometric` gives symmetric abstract patterns. All three go through the same finalize step (connectivity, reachable start and exit). They were fuzzed with extreme params × 3 sizes × 40 seeds: no fallbacks, max about 125 ms. |
| Backdrops and abstract surfaces | `src/client/render/backdrop.ts`, `surfaces.ts`, `materials.ts` | `AreaPlan.backdrop`: color, gradient, starfield, clouds, abyss, aurora, grid and noise, with reduce-motion support. Added `SurfaceStyle.pattern`: gradient, grid, noise, lines and none. The renderer no longer assumes sky, ground or gravity. Liquid uses a matte drifting surface, and remembered walls are outlined dark blocks. |
| 4th fixture world | `content/worlds/fixture-dream.json` | "The Fourth Primary": a painterly dream with 4 layers, using islands, organic and geometric layouts. It has no humans, no gravity and no architecture. |
| GM-invented rules | `src/engine/rules.ts`, `gm.ts`, `src/schema/gm.ts` | `add_rule` / `remove_rule` GM actions. `GameState.activeRules` holds `{rule, source, since, reason}` entries, at most 5 active and at most 1 new GM rule per area. Each rule gets a harm/help impact estimate checked against a difficulty allowance. That estimate was hardened over 3 review rounds against gaming: dice clamping, scaled amounts, spawn_terrain, goal counters and spent mercy. Refusals are reported as `gm_skipped` events and never appear as log lines. |
| Difficulty | `src/engine/difficulty.ts`, `Difficulty` enum in `src/schema/gm.ts` | Four levels: gentle, normal, hard and brutal. Each level scales hostile hp and damage to the player side, natural regen, and the GM rule harm allowance. Difficulty is saved with the run, and the start-screen slider passes it to the engine. |
| Systems polish | engine | Traders list their full stock (up to 8). A `currency` effect plus on-death bounties in every fixture let the player earn money. Fixtures have `startCurrency`, actors can have `persuadeDifficulty`, and loss reasons name the killer and the damage type. |
| Sim and balance | `src/engine/sim/`, `src/engine/balance/`, `tools/sim.ts` | 4 bots, a fast in-place step, and a scripted GM (`--gm`). Runs are played at the requested difficulty. Static budget analysis, plus verdicts that take sample size into account (90% Wilson interval vs the band). `autoRebalance`/`tuneWorld` use 60 seeds with fresh-block re-checks. |
| Client and UI | `src/client/ui/`, `gm-director.ts`, `view.ts` | Rules panel with a GM badge, a "New rule" toast and pill tooltips. A goal tracker with a separate hint line, a danger display and folded repeated log lines. Start options (difficulty, GM). The dialogue panel docks beside the speaker. Trade hides the Sell column when the trader buys nothing. Other fixes: exit marker avoids the panels, XP bar is clamped with a "+N levels" badge, status labels follow their actor, consumables ask before use at full vitals, and Arc Weld aims by direction. |

That spec plays each world through the real keyboard UI and asserts 0 page or console errors.

Fixes made by the gate itself:
- The last 2 open review repros (tests/review/m2/round1 and round2). `src/client/gm-director.ts` now checks action types with `Object.hasOwn` (`isGmActionType`, `actionCost`). Before, `'constructor'`/`'toString'` actions passed `sanitizeResponse`, and `responseCost` returned NaN for them.
- `SimStats.gmSkipped` now counts `gm_skipped` events. It was always 0 because it still matched the old system log lines. New test: `tests/sim/gm-skipped.test.ts`.
- `e2e/client-m2.spec.ts` "GM rules…" injected bare RuleVariants into `activeRules`, and the engine crashed in `switchesOf`. It now injects engine-shaped `{rule, source, since}` entries.

All 39 files `issues/M2-*.md` are `fixed`, some with follow-up notes (see Known issues).

## Known issues / carried into M3

- **Scripted GM makes ship/western easier**: 58→84% and 40→64%. The scripted GM's offers and rules lean helpful. The real LLM GM needs the same budget/harm discipline in the other direction, so a "helpful" ceiling, not just a harm allowance.
- **Static drain estimate overstated**: dream area 3 is predicted at 122 rule damage, and ship Ductworks at 119, which the sim does not confirm. The `--analyse` warnings ("add healing…") for those areas are noise for now. The verdict, which is sim-based, is correct.
- **Floating status labels still overlap** over the player when statuses tick on consecutive steps (dream). They follow their actor now but are not stacked.
- **Status-death loss line** names only the damage type ("put in the dirt (Heat)"), not the status (Parched).
- **No currency sink beyond traders**: there is no engine sell action, so currency piles up late in a run.
- **Bot interaction spam**: the explorer bot re-uses non-progress interactables ("Nothing happens. ×4" on teen lockers, visible in final-fixture-teen-2-mid). The log folds the lines, but the bot heuristic could skip spent interactables.
- **Test-environment caveats**: (1) editing engine/sim sources while Playwright runs reloads pages through vite HMR (the playtest specs import `src/engine/sim`), which causes spurious "execution context destroyed" failures. (2) The mapgen timing assertion (organic < 50 ms mean) fails under CPU contention, for example when vitest runs alongside e2e. (3) The client-m2 hover-route-dots check was flaky once under load.
- The M1 carry-overs on bundle size (three.js chunk > 500 kB) and the scrollbar on the prologue card during typing are unchanged.
- New requirements logged by the user during M2 (DESIGN §6b/§7b/§12): languages (Norwegian at minimum) and mobile/responsive. Both are scheduled for M3–M5.
