## Known limits (honest)

- Deaths dominate the new-player experience, even on Gentle (desktop playtest round 3: 1 win in 10
  runs). The gate bot passes, but a human new to roguelikes will mostly lose the first runs.
- The balance and win-rate figures come from bots; bots hoard and circle kiters.
- Nothing was run against a live LLM, a real phone, or real speakers (see RETROSPECTIVE.md).
- The balance step can take minutes on hostile "swarm" worlds (review round 2); it is bounded by the
  generation deadline, not fast.
- The mapgen timing assertions are load-sensitive (they passed in this gate's full vitest run).
- `docs/archive/progress/M5/` holds ~1180 review screenshots (~410 MB); a later cleanup could prune them.
